Everything You Need on Game Day

Built for coaches and team managers who want to spend less time on logistics and more time on the field.

⚾

Inning-by-Inning Lineups

Assign every player to a position for each inning. Drag and drop to rearrange your batting order on the fly.

📋

Game Tracking

Create games with opponent, date, location, and score. View your full schedule organized by season.

👥

Roster Management

Player profiles with photos, numbers, nicknames, preferred positions, and contact info. Players can be shared across teams.

🎵

Walkup Music

Set walkup songs for each player. Play them live during games with the built-in music player and Apple Music integration.

🏆

Opponent Scouting

Track head-to-head records and history against every opponent. See your win/loss breakdown at a glance.

📅

Season & Calendar Sync

Organize games by season and sync your schedule to your device calendar. Never miss a game.

🌎

Multiple Teams

Manage multiple teams from one app. Each team has its own roster, games, seasons, and settings.

☁️

iCloud Sync

Your data syncs seamlessly across all your devices via iCloud. Your lineup is always up to date.

🥎

Baseball & Softball Ready

Built for both baseball and softball with support for a customizable 10th fielder position, so your lineup fits your league's rules.
