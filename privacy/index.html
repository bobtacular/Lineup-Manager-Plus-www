Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

Lineup Manager+ ("the App") is developed by Bob Morse. This privacy policy explains how the App handles your information.

Data Storage

All data you enter into Lineup Manager+ — including teams, players, games, lineups, seasons, and saved locations — is stored locally on your device and, if enabled, synced via Apple's iCloud service to your other personal devices.

We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your data. Your information stays on your devices and in your personal iCloud account.

Data We Do Not Collect

Lineup Manager+ does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Location data (location features use Apple's on-device services)
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Photos (photos you add for teams or players are stored locally and in your iCloud)

Device Permissions

The App may request the following optional permissions:

  • Camera & Photo Library: Used to take or select photos for team and player profiles. Photos are stored on your device and in your iCloud account only.
  • Location Services: Used to search for and display game locations on a map. Location data is processed on your device using Apple's MapKit framework and is not transmitted to us.
  • Apple Music: Used to play walkup songs during games. Music access is handled entirely through Apple's MusicKit framework. We do not access your Apple Music account data.
  • Calendar: Used to sync game schedules to your device calendar. Calendar data is managed through Apple's EventKit framework and stays on your device.

iCloud

If you have iCloud enabled, the App uses Apple's CloudKit to sync your data across your devices. This data is stored in your personal iCloud account and is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

Third-Party Services

Lineup Manager+ does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The App uses only Apple's first-party frameworks (MapKit, MusicKit, CloudKit, EventKit).

Children's Privacy

Lineup Manager+ does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it is suitable for use by people of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at support@lineupmanager.plus.
